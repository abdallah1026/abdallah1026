## About Me

I am an AI engineer and researcher working across Artificial Intelligence,
Data Science, Natural Language Processing, and Agentic AI Systems Engineering.

My work focuses on building intelligent systems, developing machine learning
solutions, exploring language-model architectures, and engineering workflows
that combine reasoning, tools, data, and automation.

I created **Qorva Architecture**, an AI architecture research initiative

architectures.

I am particularly interested in the intersection of foundational AI research,
reproducible experimentation, and production-oriented AI engineering.
